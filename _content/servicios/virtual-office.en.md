## What a virtual office is

A virtual office means having your company at a business centre without occupying an office there. You register your company or your freelance activity at the centre's address, reception receives your mail and your visitors, and your phone is answered by a person in your company's name. To anyone looking for you, your company is on Calle Velázquez in Madrid, Paseo de la Independencia in Zaragoza or Calle Larios in Málaga. For you, you carry on working from wherever you work.

It is the solution for small companies and freelancers who do not want their registered address to be their home, for entrepreneurs and start-ups that need a serious address before they need an office, for professionals who work remotely, and for companies that want a presence in another city without opening a physical branch. It is available at {{ n.smart_office }} of the network's {{ g.espacios }} spaces, in all {{ g.ciudades }} cities.

## What it includes

**Registered address.** The centre's address as your company's registered, official and trading address. You can use it with the tax authority and the Companies Register, on your website, on your invoices and in your correspondence.

**Mail, parcels and notifications.** Reception receives everything that arrives in your name — letters, parcels, registered post, notifications — and lets you know straight away. You collect it whenever you like or, if you prefer, it is forwarded to you.

**Call answering.** If you take it, your calls are answered by the centre's reception in your company's name and transferred to you on the spot, or a message is taken. It is what makes a one-person company look like a company with an office.

**Visitor reception.** When a client comes to see you, reception welcomes them and shows them to the room you have booked.

**Offices, rooms and hot desks.** As a virtual office client you book offices and meeting rooms by the hour at your centre, and you have workstations at the network's centres when you are in another city. That is what sets a virtual office inside a business centre apart from a mere PO box with a name.

## Smart Office: what you want, how you want it, when you want it {: #smart-office }

Smart Office is OficinasYA!'s modular formula, available at {{ n.smart_office }} of the network's {{ g.espacios }} spaces. It starts from the virtual office — registered and trading address, mail, call answering — and adds whatever your business needs at any given time: office hours, meeting rooms, a workstation in the shared space, a phone number answered in your company's name.

The difference from contracting each thing separately is that the services go up and down month by month. One month you need calls answered and mail received; the next, three meetings in a room and two mornings in an office; the one after, nothing but the address. You add and remove services as often as you need without changing contract or centre. It is the option for people who work from home or on the move, for newly created companies, and for anyone opening a branch in a city without yet knowing how much space they will need. At some centres the pack includes meeting-room hours every month or has a reduced annual rate; check the details of the centre you are interested in. And if one day the office by the hour is no longer enough, the move to a [monthly office]({{ urls['srv-despachos'] }}) happens at the same centre.

## How to sign up

1. **Choose the centre.** It can be the one in your city or the one in the city where you want a presence. Each city page has the exact address and the reception hours, which are the hours during which calls and visitors are attended.
2. **Call {{ g.telefono }} or write** through the form. We confirm the centre's fee and the services you want to add.
3. **Sign and use the address from that day.** The contract runs month to month, with the usual company or freelancer documentation. From the moment you sign, reception is already receiving your mail and your calls.
