## A centre on Calle Mayor, with 24-hour access

The OficinasYA! centre in Albacete is at Calle Mayor 22, on the mezzanine floor, next to the city's commercial and financial district. It is a self-service centre, with access 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For a freelancer or small company in Albacete that means working whenever suits and receiving people at a central address; for a company from elsewhere, having an office and an address in the city without opening a branch.

[[cifras]]

## What the centre offers

Professional offices for 1 to 4 people, 10 to 47 m², furnished and with internet, rented by the hour or by the month with no minimum term and everything included. A 16-person meeting room with screen and whiteboard, booked by the hour, by the half-day or with packs of hours. And a virtual office: registered address, receipt of mail and notice of urgent post, for anyone who needs the address rather than the space.

It is the network's only centre in Castilla-La Mancha.
