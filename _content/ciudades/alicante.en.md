## A centre next to Alicante's official bodies

The OficinasYA! centre in Alicante is at Plaza de la Montañeta 4, in the administrative, financial and commercial centre of the city, next to the official bodies. It is easy to find and to reach: there is public parking in the area and the Renfe railway station is about ten minutes away. For a professional office or for a visitor from out of town, it is an address that needs no explaining.

## What the centre offers

Exterior offices with natural light, individual air conditioning, fibre optic and WiFi, from 12 to 20 m², equipped and ready to work from the same day, by the hour or by the month. Two meeting rooms, for 10 and 18 people, with projector and whiteboard, hired from one hour upwards: to meet clients or suppliers, for a recruitment process, a training session or a product presentation. And a virtual office, with the Smart Office pack and an extended pack of the centre's own, for anyone who needs an address, mail and an answered phone in Alicante plus a few office and room hours a month.

Reception is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 19:00. The centre's idea, in its own words, is that the client only has to worry about their business: the premises are taken care of.
