## A nearly 2,000 m² building in the centre, with parking

The OficinasYA! centre in Valencia occupies the 5th and 6th floors of a nearly 2,000 m² building at Calle Guillem de Castro 9, right in the centre of the city, with a public parking service in the building itself. It is a large centre, with offices, eight meeting and training rooms, break areas and a vending service, and with two details of its own: a prime spot for your company logo in the lobby and IT technicians at the centre for problems with your equipment.

## What the centre offers

Offices of 14 to 24 m², with functional furniture and electricity, water, cleaning, community charges and internet included, by the hour or by the month, and with preferential rates on the centre's other services for being a client. Eight spaces for meetings and training, from the small room for an interview to the classroom for a course or a conference, with projector, computer, speakers and whiteboard, and catering on request; the largest seats 28. And a virtual office: registered, official and/or trading address, mail and visitors, personalised call answering, mail forwarding and diary management.

Reception is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 14:00 and, Monday to Thursday, also from 15:15 to 19:00.
