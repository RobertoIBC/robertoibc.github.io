A centre with its own phone line in every office

The OficinasYA! centre in Castellón is at Calle Dean Martí 46, on the mezzanine floor, in Castellón de la Plana. The centre spells out precisely what comes with each office: full furniture, cabled voice and data points, air conditioning and heating, and an extension and phone number of its own per office. Reception answers calls in your company's name, receives and distributes mail and welcomes your visitors, so a one-person company operates externally like a company with an office.

What the centre offers

Offices from 6 to 30 m², from the individual to the small team, by the hour or by the month, with use of the coffee area, the daily press and community charges included in the fee. A 20-person meeting room and an 18-person training room with projector, booked by the hour. And a virtual office: registered office at the centre, use of the postal address on cards and documents, mail, visitors and answered calls, discounted if paid for a year.

Reception is open from 8:30 to 18:00. Twenty-four-hour access exists, on request, for anyone who needs it. There is parking available near the offices.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Castellón

What does an office at the Castellón centre include?

Full furniture (desk, chair, visitor chairs and filing cabinets with shelves), voice and data points with network cabling, air conditioning and heating, use of the coffee area, the daily press and community charges. Each office also has its own extension and phone number, and the centre's address as registered office.

Can I get into the Castellón centre outside reception hours?

Reception is open from 8:30 to 18:00 and 24-hour access is available on request: if you are going to need to come in during the evening or at weekends regularly, ask for it when signing up. There is parking available near the offices.

Is there a training room in Castellón?

Yes. As well as the 20-person meeting room, the centre has an 18-person training room with projector, in classroom layout. It is booked by the hour and you do not need to be a client of the centre. It is the space for courses, client sessions or in-person exams.

Is the Castellón virtual office discounted?

Yes, for annual payment. The virtual office includes the centre's address as registered office, use of the postal address on cards and documents, receipt of mail and visitors and calls answered in your company's name. If it is contracted and paid for a year, the centre applies a discount; ask for the figure on 918 298 500.
