Frequently asked questions

What people always ask us

OficinasYA! is a network of 35 business centres and coliving spaces in 17 cities across Spain, offering private offices, meeting rooms, coworking and virtual office services.

How much does it cost to rent a private office by the hour?

Renting a private office by the hour at OficinasYA! starts at €8.50 per hour plus VAT: every price on this site is shown before tax. It includes the equipped office, high-speed WiFi and visitor reception, and there is no minimum commitment. It is the usual choice for one-off meetings or occasional work at any of the 35 locations in the network.

What does the virtual office include and how much is it?

The OficinasYA! virtual office starts at €20 a month plus VAT and includes a registered and trading address, mail handling and optional call answering. It lets you register a company and present a professional image without occupying physical space, which is why freelancers and remote companies use it most.

Which Spanish cities has OficinasYA! got centres in?

OficinasYA! has 35 centres and coliving spaces across 17 Spanish cities: Madrid, Barcelona, Bilbao, Seville, Valencia, Murcia, Tenerife, Malaga, A Coruña, Vigo, Zaragoza, Salamanca, Segovia, Castellón, Alicante, Albacete and Mérida. Madrid has 8 centres, and Barcelona, Murcia, Seville and Tenerife have 3 each. They all belong to the same network, so you deal with a single provider.

Can I access the offices 24 hours a day?

In Madrid yes, at all eight centres. Outside Madrid it is available at Albacete, both Galia buildings in Seville, both Tenerife centres and both colivings, and Castellón offers it on request. Everywhere else reception closes in the afternoon, between 15:00 and 21:00 depending on the centre. Check the opening hours of the one you are interested in before signing up.

How long does it take to get the office up and running?

The space is handed over equipped and ready to use, with no building works or fit-out beforehand, so you can start working the same day you sign. That is the main difference from renting a conventional unit, where fitting out takes months. To check availability, just call +34 918 298 500.

What is the difference between hourly and monthly offices?

With the hourly office you pay only for the time you use, from €8.50 an hour plus VAT, and it creates no commitment. The monthly option, from €270 a month plus VAT, gives you an exclusive private office with your own key and 24/7 access in Madrid and selected centres, and includes company registration, cleaning and 5 hours of meeting room a month.
